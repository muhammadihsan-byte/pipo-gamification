# Pipo mascot identity

Pipo V4 is the exclusive approved mascot throughout Pipo. Derive every pose, expression, perspective and animation from the approved V4 master. Preserve its body proportions, eyes, snout, floppy ears, flower, hooves, tail, colors, shading and rounded rendering.

Reuse the approved V4 assets and the shared motion renderer. Use the V4 neutral still when a requested motion is unavailable. Legacy Pipo artwork (V1–V3), generic pig illustrations, substitute characters and independently redrawn masters must never represent Pipo.
